A note from Kristina

Beauty can be holy.

For years I poured my heart into retail, helping build a family-run boutique that became a beloved part of our community. But something deeper was always stirring: a longing to create a space where faith wasn’t just part of the brand, it was the reason for it.

Eden Earth was born from that longing. Through curated goods, slow fashion, clean beauty, timeless gifting and Word-centered resources, I pray it becomes more than something you shop, and something you become a part of.

You’re not just welcome here. You’re wanted.

Kristina

The mission

“Eden Earth is dedicated to cultivating meaningful living through thoughtfully curated goods, spiritual resources, and community engagement, fostering discipleship, biblical education, and outreach for individuals living rooted, purposeful lives.”

Our mission in three movements

Acceptance becomes identity. Identity becomes destiny.

  1. I

    Root

    We root you in truth.

    A place of acceptance where the finished work of Jesus meets your story, and Scripture becomes the ground beneath your feet.

  2. II

    Restore

    We restore your identity.

    We walk with you into healing and deliverance, so that who God says you are becomes louder than what you have carried.

  3. III

    Release

    We release you to bloom.

    A tended life bears fruit for others. We commission you into your calling, sent out to replant Eden wherever your feet land.
